# NILAM PESUM - Scientific Limitations & Operational Constraints

1. **Satellite Spatial Resolution**:
   - NASA POWER has a native grid resolution of $0.5^\circ \times 0.625^\circ$ (~50 km).
   - NASA SMAP has a spatial resolution of 9 km (L3 enhanced) and 36 km (L3 passive).
   - NASA MODIS has a resolution of 250 meters.
   - Observations reflect pixel aggregations for the region and do not replace micro-scale laboratory ground sampling.

2. **Cloud Cover & Optical Gaps**:
   - NASA MODIS (MOD13Q1) and HLS optical sensors are subject to cloud occlusion during heavy monsoon downpours. Fmask and pixel quality flags are used to filter contaminated pixels.

3. **Soil Moisture vs Chemical Testing**:
   - NASA SMAP measures volumetric water content ($\text{cm}^3/\text{cm}^3$) using L-band radiometry. It does not measure soil pH, chemical nitrogen, phosphorus, or potassium. Chemical soil testing must be entered by farmers from their Soil Health Card.

4. **Reanalysis vs Direct Observation**:
   - NASA MERRA-2 is a modern atmospheric reanalysis coupling spaceborne observations with general circulation modeling, not a direct ground station measurement.

5. **Simulated Scenarios vs Forecasts**:
   - What-if climate stress tests (Category 4) evaluate hypothetical temperature or rainfall variations. They are strictly agronomic sensitivity analyses and must not be interpreted as meteorological forecasts.

6. **Suitability Scores vs Harvest Yield**:
   - Crop suitability scores reflect environmental and agronomic favorability; they do not guarantee final crop yield or financial profits.
